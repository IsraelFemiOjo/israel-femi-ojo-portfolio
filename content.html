Content strategy

Research, storytelling and distribution built around what people really resonate with.

I research the topic, find an entry point that fits the brand and shape the content for its audience. The work below includes original posts, video and replies.

My workflow

  1. 01 Research

    Find the topic and check the facts.

  2. 02 Brand angle

    Find a brand-specific entry point. Shape the story.

  3. 03 Format

    Create a post, video or reply.

  4. 04 Publish

    Edit, get approval where needed and publish.

  5. 05 Distribute

    Join relevant conversations.

  6. 06 Measure

    Review engagement and profile visits.

  7. 07 Refine

    Revise the content where needed.

This is a typical sequence. The steps can overlap or happen in a different order.
