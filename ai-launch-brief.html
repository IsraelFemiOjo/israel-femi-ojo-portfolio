AI launch brief

Objective

Test whether AI could prepare a reliable launch package from conflicting documents.

What I did

  • Created six internal documents for a fictional startup.
  • Planted ten conflicts across those documents.
  • Gave three models the same task and evaluated claim verification, editorial judgement and conflict detection.

Results

  • GPT 5.6 Sol Max scored 97/100. It also spotted a tier-name mismatch missing from the answer key.
  • Fable 5 Max scored 92/100. It presented one disputed claim as confirmed.
  • Grok 4.5 Speed scored 80/100. It presented several disputed claims as confirmed.

Conclusion

GPT handled the conflicting documents most accurately in this test. The comparison used one scenario and one run per model.
