AI evaluation · 2026

AI launch brief

3 models. 6 company documents. 10 planted conflicts.

RoleTest design and review
ModelsGrok, Fable and GPT
ResultGPT scored 97/100

The test

Could the models keep disputed claims out of launch copy?

Each model received the same six documents and the same prompt. The contradictions covered pricing, security, performance, support and customer claims.

Main finding

Two models found conflicts, then repeated some of them as facts.

That makes the brief unsafe to publish without checking the source documents again.

Scores

GPT 5.6 Sol Max performed best. It also caught a tier-name mismatch that was missing from the answer key.

Takeaway

Finding a conflict is not enough.

The model must also keep that claim out of anything marked ready to publish.

This was one fictional company, one task and one run per model. The scores describe this test only.
