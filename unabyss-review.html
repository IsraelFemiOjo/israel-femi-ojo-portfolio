Unabyss review

Review objective

Check whether Unabyss could retain context for the different accounts I manage without mixing their information or brand voices.

Independent product review.

Challenges discovered

Observations

  • Say when no content was found and show which filters are active.
  • Do not mark setup complete while the memory vault is empty. Show the next step.
  • Do not claim time saved when the memory count is zero.
  • Import the page contents, not just database labels and titles.

Conclusion

The import issues prevented me from testing context separation. Marcin reviewed the import logs, confirmed the findings and said the engineering team was working on fixes.
