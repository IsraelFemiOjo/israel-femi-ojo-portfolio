What I tested

Could Unabyss keep two brand voices separate?

I connected a fresh Notion workspace and planned to switch between two very different brand profiles. The import failed before that test could begin.

What happened

01Notion connected.
02Setup said “All set.”
03Three answers said “Restricted.”
04The memory count stayed at zero.

What I found

01

“Restricted” described the wrong problem.

The information was not blocked. It had never been imported.

02

“All set” appeared too early.

The connection was complete. The import was not.

03

“Time saved” had no result behind it.

The dashboard showed saved time while the memory count was zero.

04

The profile came from metadata.

It described sample rows and schema, not the notes inside the pages.

What I sent

Four fixes.

Name the real import state.

Wait for content before showing “All set.”

Hide time saved until something is retrieved.

Import page content, not only titles and schema.

Founder’s reply

The founder checked the logs and confirmed the issues.

He said the integration was already being fixed. The original context-separation test is still unfinished.
