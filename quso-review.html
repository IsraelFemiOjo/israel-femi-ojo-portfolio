Quso review

Review objective

Review whether Quso’s homepage makes it clear who the product is for and why someone should choose it.

Independent review.

Challenges discovered

The opening mainly presents video clipping. Editing, scheduling and publishing appear further down, so the first screen does little to explain why someone would use these tools together.

Observations

Quso’s published ReeAnimate customer story discussed both clip selection and scheduling. This suggested that the wider workflow deserved attention, although one customer’s experience was not enough to decide the main message.

Recommendations

  • Start with existing customer feedback to identify who gets the most value from Quso.
  • Ask customers why they signed up, which features they rely on and which alternatives they considered.
  • Use those findings to decide which audience and benefit the homepage should address first.
  • Support the message with relevant customer examples on the website and social pages.

Conclusion

The homepage should explain the reason to choose Quso before introducing the wider feature set. Customer research would help establish which reason deserves to lead.
